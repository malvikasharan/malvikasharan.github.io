Jul 2021

The Alan Turing Institute
Senior Researcher, Tools, Practices, and Systems

I established and led a team of 10 Research Community Managers (RCMs), with six associated members, at the Turing, the UK’s national institute for data science. RCMs are integral to fostering communities of practice, establishing cross-project and cross-team collaborations, and driving the adoption and implementation of data science best practices. Funded under the Tools, Practices, and Systems research program, established from initial investment through the £38 million AI for Science and Government investment, I was the second member of this team tasked with developing and pioneering research community management approaches both at the institution and through international collaborative initiatives.

I developed practices, infrastructure, and resources and operationalized community management at the institute by mentoring, training, and championing the work of RCMs who, alongside Research Software Engineers, Research Applications Managers, and Data Wranglers, contribute to high-performing data science and AI projects.

Feb 2023
– Mar 2025
Project Lead—The Turing Way Practitioners Hub

I established The Turing Way Practitioners Hub as a platform for cross-sector collaboration, knowledge exchange, and strategic partnerships, spanning various sectors and data science organizations, including small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

Funded under BridgeAI, a £100 million investment by Innovate UK, I piloted the cohort-based model for strategic engagement and cross-sector knowledge sharing through partnerships with national organizations such as the Office for National Statistics, Genomics England, and the British Antarctic Survey.

I led the program for two cohorts, working with 40+ companies across sectors and openly publishing 15 case studies and resources on Zenodo to support strategic roadmaps for adopting AI, guided by open source principles, open data practices, and ethical frameworks. The program has since evolved into a core BridgeAI offering, currently operating as the Practitioners Hub: AI Deployment Studio.

Jul 2021
– Jun 2025
Principal Investigator—The Turing Way

I led The Turing Way, an open-source, community-led project focused on data science best practices. For over five years, I have guided the development and scaling of a handbook dedicated to open, reproducible, and ethical research practices, alongside nurturing a global research community.

Accessed by over 84,000 unique visitors annually, The Turing Way has been co-created by 500 international collaborators, who share research practices, guidance, and recommendations through chapters across five guides and through community-based collaboration. This body of work has inspired high-impact projects and organizations, including the NHS, NASA, the Office for National Statistics, and ELIXIR, and is referenced in 100+ peer-reviewed articles, training materials, and policy documents (some named in this 2024 publication).

2025
– Present
RCM Cooperative, Co-founder. This member-led cooperative supports the professionalization of Research Community Managers and helps research communities become more equitable, resilient, and collaborative.

Co-founded a member-led cooperative with two former Alan Turing Institute colleagues to advance the professionalization of Research Community Managers (RCMs) and build equitable, resilient, and collaborative research communities. The cooperative has built a democratic, multi-tier membership structure and launched a public Monthly Seminar series in September 2026 (attracting 100 registrations at launch) alongside open-licensed tools for sustainable community building. Key engagements include delivering RCM101 training for Fellows of the Institute for Research Software and the BioFAIR community, leading to UKRI DisCouRSE-funded commissions for “RCM for Leaders” training and research into hidden community leaders, and co-creating operating principles with ADR UK that supported the funded launch of the UK Data Linkage Community.
